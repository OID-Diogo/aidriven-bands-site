The problem

Managing releases across platforms is time-consuming

Independent artists juggle publishing schedules, caption writing, platform-specific formats and engagement tracking across multiple apps. AI Driven Bands brings it all into one place.

📅

Scheduled publishing

Set publish times for Instagram Feed, Reels, Stories, TikTok videos and YouTube uploads in a single calendar view. Posts go out automatically at the configured time.

🎵

Release campaigns

Group all content for a single release into one campaign — teaser, release day post, lyric video, behind-the-scenes — and manage them together.

📊

Performance dashboard

Track reach, plays and engagement after each post. Correlate social performance with streaming numbers from Spotify for Artists and Last.fm in one view.

🔒

Secure OAuth connections

Connect Instagram, TikTok and YouTube through each platform's official authorization flow. No passwords stored. Access tokens encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM.

Who it's for

Built for independent artists and their teams

🎸

Independent artists

Artists who release music themselves and need a reliable way to coordinate posting across Instagram, TikTok and YouTube without hiring a full marketing team.

🎯

Band managers

Managers handling multiple acts who need to schedule content in advance, track post performance, and maintain consistent publishing cadences across releases.

🏷️

Small labels

Indie labels managing several artist rosters who want a centralized tool for campaign coordination instead of spreadsheets and separate platform dashboards.
